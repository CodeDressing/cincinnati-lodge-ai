About Cincinnati Lodge No. III

Brotherhood, Service, Leadership, and Community

Cincinnati Lodge No. III is connected to the long tradition of Freemasonry in Morristown, New Jersey. The Lodge represents brotherhood, integrity, charity, education, leadership, personal improvement, and meaningful community presence.

Brotherhood

A fraternity rooted in trust, friendship, character, and lifelong bonds.

Service

A commitment to charity, responsibility, and supporting the broader community.

Growth

A path toward self-improvement, education, leadership, and moral development.
